# Maṇipūra image placeholders

Place at most eight images in your app's public folder:

```text
public/assets/manipura/
  01-spark-dawn.webp
  02-jewel-city.webp
  03-navel-fire.webp
  04-inner-forge.webp
  05-human-agency.webp
  06-ram-chamber.webp
  07-inner-sun.webp
  08-fire-to-air.webp
```

The experience does not require all eight images to run. Missing images simply leave the layered procedural atmosphere visible. The same images are deliberately reused as cinematic environments with different crop, parallax, glow, particle and geometry treatments.

Do not put text into the images. Keep typography and symbolic glyphs as HTML/SVG so they stay crisp and accessible.
